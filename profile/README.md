## What We Do

**Managed Cloud & Kubernetes Infrastructure** — from Swiss-hosted cloud to private on-prem deployments, we run and maintain the platforms our customers build on.

- **[Natron Cloud](https://natron.io)** — Swiss-hosted managed cloud with 24/7 monitoring, automated scaling, and 99.9% uptime SLA
- **Natron Flex Stack** — Dedicated private cloud on your own hardware
- **Bring Your Own Cloud** — We manage your Azure, GCP, or on-premise infrastructure
- **Managed Kubernetes** — Enterprise-grade clusters with a full CNCF-native stack (Cilium, ArgoCD, Prometheus, Grafana, Velero, Cert-Manager, and more)
- **Managed Databases** — PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MSSQL with automated backups, HA, and point-in-time recovery
- **Managed Linux** — Docker, OpenVPN, DNS, Proxmox, and OS-level management
